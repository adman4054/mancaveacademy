## Step 1: Define a Clear Target

Not:

> Small businesses

Instead:

> Roofing companies with 5–20 employees  
> Dental offices under $3M revenue  
> Local service companies running Google Ads

Specific targeting increases response rate.

---

## Step 2: Choose One Primary Channel

Do not spread yourself thin.

Pick one:

- LinkedIn outreach
- Cold email
- Local networking
- Targeted ads
- Direct mail

Consistency > variety.

---

## Step 3: Build a 3-Step Outreach Sequence

Example cold email flow:

Email 1: Problem-focused introduction  
Email 2: Short follow-up  
Email 3: Case-style example

Keep it simple.

Use AI to draft and refine messaging:
→ **[AI Workflow Stack](/articles/ai-workflow-stack)**

---

## Step 4: Track Metrics Weekly

Track:

- messages sent
- responses
- calls booked
- close rate

Improve what moves conversion.

Ignore vanity metrics.

---

## Step 5: Automate After It Works

Only automate once you have:

- positive response rate
- booked calls
- closed deals

Automation without proof wastes time.
