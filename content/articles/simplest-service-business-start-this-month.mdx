If you want fast cash flow, build a service.

Not a brand.
Not a course.
Not a SaaS product.

A focused service that solves one painful problem.

If you’re new to this path:
→ **[Local Leverage](/local-leverage)**

---

## Step 1: Pick a Painful, Recurring Problem

Look for:

- businesses losing time
- businesses losing money
- businesses making repetitive mistakes

Examples:

- website maintenance for small businesses
- local SEO cleanup
- AI workflow setup for small agencies
- lead follow-up automation
- reporting automation

The key:

Recurring pain.
Clear outcome.

---

## Step 2: Package It Simply

Do not sell “services.”

Sell outcomes.

Instead of:

> Marketing consulting

Say:

> 30-day Local SEO Reset for Service Businesses

Clarity converts.

---

## Step 3: Set a Simple Price

Avoid hourly billing.

Offer:

- flat fee
- clear scope
- defined timeline

Example:

$1,500 for a 30-day SEO reset.

That’s easier to sell than “$95/hour.”

---

## Why This Works

Service businesses:

- generate fast cash
- require low startup cost
- validate real demand
- build operator skill

They fund asset building.

Not replace it.

---

## The Goal

Build cash flow.
Build systems.
Then build assets.

That’s leverage.
