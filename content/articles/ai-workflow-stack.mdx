## The 3-Layer AI Stack

Keep it simple:

1. Writing & thinking layer  
2. Storage & knowledge layer  
3. Execution layer  

That’s it.

---

## 1️⃣ Writing & Thinking Layer

Pick one primary AI tool.

It should handle:

- drafting
- summarizing
- outlining
- restructuring ideas
- rewriting for clarity

Use it for:

- email drafts
- proposal templates
- content outlines
- offer positioning
- research summaries

Don’t switch tools constantly.
Depth > novelty.

This is your leverage engine.

## 2️⃣ Storage & Knowledge Layer

AI becomes powerful when connected to structured information.

You need:

- one note system
- one structured folder system
- one archive for prompts/templates

Examples:

- store reusable prompts
- save proposal frameworks
- keep offer positioning documents
- track refined workflow prompts

Think of this as your “operating manual.”

Without this layer, AI becomes disposable.

---

## 3️⃣ Execution Layer

This is where leverage compounds.

Your execution layer includes:

- task manager
- calendar
- CRM (if service-based)
- simple automation where useful

AI should plug into these systems, not replace them.

Example:

- Draft proposal with AI  
- Move it into task system  
- Send  
- Follow up with templated reminder  

That’s a workflow.

---

## Example: Weekly Content Workflow

Instead of “writing when inspired”:

1. Outline article with AI
2. Expand sections
3. Refine tone
4. Publish
5. Repurpose summary for newsletter

Repeat weekly.

That’s output discipline.

## Avoid Tool Chaos

Common mistake:

- 5 writing AIs
- 3 note apps
- 7 automations
- zero structure

Instead:

- 1 primary AI
- 1 note system
- 1 task system

You scale by improving workflows, not stacking subscriptions.
