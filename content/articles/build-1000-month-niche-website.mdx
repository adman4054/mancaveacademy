A niche website is one of the simplest digital assets you can build.

Not a personal brand.
Not a content empire.

A focused property solving one category of problems.

If structured correctly, a niche site can:

- generate affiliate revenue
- sell simple digital products
- attract inbound leads
- become a sellable asset

---

## Step 1: Choose a Problem Category (Not a Broad Topic)

Bad example:

> Fitness

Better example:

> At-home strength training for men over 40

Even better:

> Knee-friendly strength programs for men over 40

The narrower the starting point, the easier the authority.

---

## Step 2: Validate Search Demand

Before building:

- search for long-tail keywords
- look for weak competition
- identify recurring questions
- check if products already exist

You’re not chasing traffic.

You’re identifying commercial intent.

---

## Step 3: Build 10 Foundational Articles

Start with:

- 3 buyer-intent articles
- 4 problem-solving guides
- 3 comparison pieces

Don’t publish randomly.

Structure content around:

- high intent
- clear problems
- monetizable outcomes

If AI helps with production:
→ **[AI Leverage](/ai-leverage)**

---

## Step 4: Monetize Early

You don’t need massive traffic.

You need:

- focused readers
- relevant offers
- aligned products

Options:

- affiliate products
- simple digital templates
- lead generation
- service upsells

The goal is signal, not perfection.

---

## Step 5: Improve What Works

Once traffic starts:

- expand high-performing topics
- update winning posts
- improve conversion points
- add email capture

Think like an operator.

Not a blogger.
